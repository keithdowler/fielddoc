# FieldDoc Product Copy

## Positioning

FieldDoc helps field teams document work, preserve original evidence, and generate Proof Packets that make completed work easier to verify, bill, and defend.

## Short Description

Document the job. Prove the work. FieldDoc keeps job photos, notes, files, and Proof Packet reports organized on your device first, then backs them up when you are ready.

## Value Propositions

- Capture Before, Work, and After evidence in a structured field workflow.
- Keep original files and proof metadata intact.
- Generate a customer-ready Proof Packet from local evidence.
- Work offline at the job site, then sync when connection and subscription are ready.
- Preserve conflicting edits instead of silently discarding field work.
- Block unsafe supporting files before they reach customer delivery.

- Job proof, photos, reports
- Field evidence and reports

## Onboarding Copy

Start with a job name. Add site and customer details when you have them. Capture Before, Work, and After proof as the job moves forward. Generate a Proof Packet when the evidence is ready.

## Error Copy Standards

- Say what failed.
- Say what is still safe.
- Say the next action.

Example: "Subscription check failed. Local evidence is still saved on this device. Refresh after connection is restored."
